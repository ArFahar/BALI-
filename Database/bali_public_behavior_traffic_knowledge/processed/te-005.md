# TE-005 — Parking and Local Access Etiquette in Canggu

**Category:** `traffic_etiquette/parking_local_access_etiquette`  
**Location scope:** Canggu-focused visitor guidance; authorized parking locations are site-specific and may change  
**Verification:** partially_verified (medium)

## Overview
Guidance for choosing parking spaces and keeping local roads, entrances, and community access routes clear in Canggu.

## Cultural and local context
Badung Regency has a regional regulation on parking facilities and a later amendment identifying public-road and special parking locations. This confirms that parking is managed through designated arrangements rather than being an unrestricted right to leave vehicles anywhere. The exact legal parking locations and current arrangements must be checked locally.

## Why it matters
Many Canggu streets and access lanes are narrow and serve residents, businesses, deliveries, pedestrians, and emergency vehicles. Poorly parked vehicles can obstruct daily life and create safety risks.

## Visitor guidance

### Do
- Use clearly marked or venue-authorized parking areas
- Ask venue staff where customers should park before leaving a vehicle
- Check signs, road markings, and instructions from parking attendants
- Keep gates, driveways, temple access, sidewalks, and narrow lanes unobstructed
- Take note of where the vehicle is parked and follow the operator's payment instructions

### Don't
- Do not assume an empty roadside space is legal or available for parking
- Do not park across a gate, driveway, sidewalk, or narrow road
- Do not block access for residents, deliveries, emergency services, or ceremonial activity
- Do not leave a vehicle in a location marked as prohibited or reserved

## Common misconceptions
- A space being physically empty does not mean it is an authorized parking space.
- A cafe or accommodation recommendation does not guarantee that roadside parking is permitted.
- Parking arrangements can change due to events, construction, ceremonies, or local traffic management.

## Local variation
- Parking locations and enforcement can vary by street, venue, desa adat, and local authority.
- This entry does not provide a live list of legal parking spaces in Canggu.

## Notes for the AI
For a legal dispute, parking fee complaint, or current designated-parking question, consult the relevant Badung government or local authority. Do not invent parking fees, availability, or enforcement rules.
