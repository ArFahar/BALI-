# Human Review Required

## Priority review queue
1. **Canang Sari:** Ask a Balinese Hindu practitioner or cultural educator to review definition, symbolic interpretation, and visitor guidance.
2. **Temple etiquette:** Ask temple management or a recognized local guide to confirm which instructions are general and which are site-specific. Check current provincial rules.
3. **Tri Hita Karana:** Ask a cultural scholar or practitioner to review the plain-language explanation and avoid oversimplification.
4. **Desa Adat / Awig-Awig / Pararem:** Legal definitions are source-supported; obtain the actual current Canggu customary documents or an authorized explanation before generating Canggu-specific answers.
5. **Galungan-Kuningan:** Ask a religious/cultural practitioner to review the meaning and obtain full academic and primary sources before adding detailed ritual explanations.

## Publication rule
Only source-supported statements may be used in answers. For local rules, sacred or restricted knowledge, and unresolved disagreement, the assistant must state uncertainty and direct users to the relevant local authority.
