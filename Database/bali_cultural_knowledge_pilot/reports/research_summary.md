# Bali Cultural Knowledge Base — Pilot Research Report

## Scope
Initial desk-research pilot for a static knowledge base supporting a Bali Cultural Assistant. Pilot topics: Tri Hita Karana, Canang Sari, sacred-place etiquette, Desa Adat/Awig-Awig/Pararem, and Galungan-Kuningan.

## Results
- Knowledge entries: 5
- Source records: 8
- Unique URLs: 9
- Verified: 1 (legal definitions in Pergub 4/2020)
- Partially verified: 4
- Conflicting: 0 identified in this limited pass
- All five entries require local expert review before production publication.

## Important limitations
1. This was a source-led web research and extraction pass, not unrestricted automated scraping.
2. No interviews were conducted. No source has been represented as locally validated.
3. Canggu-specific customary rules have NOT been established. Province-wide sources must not be used to invent village-specific rules.
4. The tourist etiquette source from Dinas Pariwisata Denpasar refers to 2023 guidance. Check the latest provincial circular and each site's current instructions before treating any item as current law.
5. The Canang Sari source is a local-government page, but it points to a secondary blog for further detail. Detailed symbolism needs corroboration.
6. The Galungan-Kuningan entry is based on an academic article record/abstract; obtain the full paper and additional sources for detailed ritual explanations.

## Recommended next research batch
- Penjor and its meanings, using official and academic sources.
- Nyepi and visitor guidance, distinguishing religious meaning from current operational restrictions.
- Odalan and temple anniversary practices.
- Balinese speech levels and respectful greetings.
- Canggu-specific Desa Adat and Banjar documentation, obtained directly from the relevant customary authority.
- Temple-specific visitor rules for actual pilot locations.

## RAG readiness
The JSON schema and pilot records are structured for later cleaning, chunking, embedding, and indexing. They should not be treated as fully production-approved until local review and source updates are complete.
