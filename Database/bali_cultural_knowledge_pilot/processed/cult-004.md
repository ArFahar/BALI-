# Desa Adat, Awig-Awig, and Pararem

- **ID:** CULT-004
- **Category:** local_governance / customary_governance
- **Scope:** general_legal_framework_with_village_specific_rules
- **Verification:** verified (high)
- **Local review required:** True

## Definition
Under Bali Governor Regulation No. 4 of 2020, Desa Adat is a customary-law community unit in Bali with its own territory, traditional structure, rights, assets, traditions, sacred-place ties, duties, authority, and right to manage its own affairs. Awig-Awig are rules made by a Desa Adat and/or Banjar Adat. Pararem are rules or decisions of a Desa Adat assembly that implement Awig-Awig, regulate new matters, or resolve customary cases.

## Cultural context
The regulation describes Desa Adat life in relation to Parahyangan, Pawongan, and Palemahan. It also identifies different community membership categories, including Krama Desa Adat, Krama Tamiu, and Tamiu.

## Significance
Customary governance helps explain why local rules and community processes can differ from one Desa Adat to another.

## Common situations
- A visitor or newcomer hears about a local customary rule
- A business operates within a customary village area
- A newcomer is advised to ask a local authority about a community event or restriction

## Visitor guidance

### Do
- Ask the relevant Desa Adat or an authorized local representative about rules affecting a specific location or activity
- Treat village-specific Awig-Awig and Pararem as geographically bounded unless the source says otherwise

### Don't
- Assume one village's customary rule applies throughout Bali
- Treat an informal online summary as a substitute for the current local rule or authorized explanation

## Misconceptions
- Awig-Awig and Pararem are not interchangeable terms in the cited regulation.

## Regional variations
- The content and application of customary rules are specific to each Desa Adat and may be supplemented by local decisions.

## Additional notes
Do not infer the specific Awig-Awig or Pararem of Desa Adat Canggu from province-wide legislation. Obtain the actual current local documents or confirmation from the relevant authority.

## Verification notes
Legal definitions are directly supported by the regulation. The actual current customary rules for Canggu have not been collected; obtain and validate local documents before giving place-specific answers.
