## Definition
Galungan and Kuningan are important celebrations in Balinese Hindu religious life. An academic article indexed by Garuda describes the Galungan-Kuningan ritual as associated with devotion to God and the victory of dharma (goodness) over adharma (evil).

## Cultural context
The cited article compares Galungan-Kuningan with Navratri and discusses similarities and differences. The entry uses only the broad meaning stated in the article abstract and does not attempt to explain every ritual sequence.

## Significance
The celebrations are religious occasions, not simply decorative or entertainment events. Visitors may encounter community preparations, offerings, and ceremonial activity.

## Common situations
- Seeing penjor or ceremonial preparations
- Experiencing changes in local business or community activity around religious days
- Encountering processions or worship

## Visitor guidance

### Do
- Ask hosts or local guides what is appropriate if you encounter a ceremony
- Respect temporary changes to access, traffic, or opening hours
- Observe from an appropriate place without disrupting worship

### Don't
- Assume all villages celebrate in exactly the same way
- Treat ceremonies as performances staged for visitors

## Misconceptions
- Galungan and Kuningan should not be reduced to a generic tourist festival; they have religious significance.
