# Basic Road Safety and Traffic Compliance

- ID: TE-002
- Category: traffic_etiquette / road_safety
- Verification: verified (high)

## Definition
Road users must obey signs and directions, use required safety equipment, respect passenger limits, and avoid impaired or distracted driving.

## Context
The 2025 circular requires valid licences, compliance with traffic rules, helmets, obedience to signs, passenger limits, and no driving under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs. Bali Police have highlighted helmet and seatbelt use, phone use while driving, wrong-way driving, underage riding, and overloading as enforcement concerns.

## Significance
Road safety protects drivers, passengers, pedestrians, cyclists, and other road users.

## Common situations
- Riding a scooter with a passenger
- Driving on busy tourist roads
- Using navigation while driving
- Approaching an intersection

## Do
- Wear a properly fastened motorcycle helmet
- Use seat belts
- Follow signs, signals, and police directions
- Stay within passenger capacity
- Stop safely before using a phone
- Never drive under the influence

## Don't
- Ride without a helmet
- Use a mobile phone while driving
- Drive against traffic
- Overload a vehicle
- Drive if underage or not legally licensed

## Misconceptions
- A short ride or quiet road does not make safety requirements optional.
- A rental provider handing over a vehicle does not transfer responsibility for safe driving.

## Additional notes
Police reporting identifies enforcement priorities, not a complete list of offences or penalties.

## Verification
The core obligations are explicit in the Governor Circular and reinforced by Bali Police communications.
