# Canggu-focused knowledge extension

Added three entries:
- TE-004: Pedestrian and Cycling Etiquette in Canggu
- PB-004: Nightlife and Neighborhood Etiquette in Canggu
- TE-005: Parking and Local Access Etiquette in Canggu

## Important evidence limits
- Sidewalk guidance comes from Denpasar Transportation Agency and is not a street-by-street Canggu inventory.
- Canggu noise references document local government coordination and customary discussions in 2022. They do not establish a universal current closing time or current legal limit for every venue.
- Badung parking regulations establish a managed parking framework, but this package does not identify a live list of designated parking spaces in Canggu.
- Cycling recommendations are safety and courtesy guidance; do not represent them as a Canggu-specific statutory cycling code.

All three entries require local review by a Canggu resident, desa adat/banjar representative, transport authority, or relevant business operator before production deployment.
