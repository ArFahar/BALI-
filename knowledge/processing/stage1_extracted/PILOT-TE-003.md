# Road Position, Parking, Pedestrians, and Accident Response

## Definition
Drivers should use the correct side of the road, signal and maneuver carefully, avoid obstructing traffic, respect pedestrians and cyclists, and respond responsibly after accidents.

## Cultural context
Kejati Bali's foreigner legal FAQ summarizes left-side driving, signaling before turns, avoiding mobile phone use, and respecting pedestrian and traffic rules. The 2025 Governor Circular requires compliance with Indonesian traffic regulations and signs.

## Significance
Predictable driving and consideration for vulnerable road users reduce conflict, especially on narrow or crowded roads.

## Common situations
- Joining traffic from a side road
- Turning or changing lanes
- Parking near shops or beaches
- Encountering pedestrians and cyclists
- Being involved in a road accident

## Do
- Drive on the left
- Signal before turning or changing direction
- Park only where permitted and do not block traffic or pedestrian paths
- Give pedestrians and cyclists space
- If an accident occurs, stop, assist where safe, and contact police or emergency services when required

## Don't
- Drive against traffic
- Park where restricted or obstructive
- Use a phone while driving
- Leave an accident scene without following legal duties

## Misconceptions
- Road etiquette is also about predictable behavior and consideration, not only avoiding fines.

## Regional variations
- Road layouts and parking controls differ by locality; follow signs, markings, and police directions.

## Notes
The Kejati FAQ is explanatory information, not a substitute for current statutory text. Accident procedures need direct current legal/police confirmation.
