# Pedestrian and Cycling Etiquette in Canggu

## Definition
Practical guidance for people walking or cycling in Canggu, where pedestrians, bicycles, motorcycles, cars, delivery vehicles, and local access needs share busy streets.

## Cultural context
Pedestrian safety and unobstructed public access are shared responsibilities. Denpasar Transportation Agency states that sidewalks are for pedestrians and should not be used for parking or motor vehicles. This is a useful Bali public-space principle, but the cited page is Denpasar-specific and does not establish the condition or legal status of every Canggu sidewalk.

## Significance
Canggu's narrow and busy streets can create conflicts between visitors, residents, businesses, and road users. Respectful movement helps preserve access and reduce avoidable risk.

## Common situations
- Walking between accommodation, cafes, beaches, and shops
- Cycling on mixed-traffic roads
- Encountering narrow or missing sidewalks
- Crossing roads near busy commercial areas

## Do
- Use a sidewalk or designated pedestrian path where one is available
- If no safe sidewalk exists, stay alert and follow applicable road rules; avoid assuming the road edge is a protected pedestrian lane
- Cross at a safe, visible point and watch for motorcycles approaching from either direction
- Cycle predictably, keep to the appropriate side of the road, use lights when visibility is poor, and wear a helmet
- Give way to pedestrians and avoid blocking entrances, drainage channels, or narrow access ways

## Don't
- Do not ride or park motorcycles on pedestrian sidewalks
- Do not cycle against traffic or weave unpredictably
- Do not assume every road in Canggu has a protected cycling lane
- Do not block village roads, temple access, shop entrances, or emergency access for photos or waiting

## Misconceptions
- A road that looks quiet is not necessarily safe for walking or cycling.
- A general sidewalk principle does not mean every Canggu street has a continuous sidewalk.
- This entry does not provide live road closures, temporary diversions, or current traffic conditions.

## Regional variations
- Sidewalk availability and road width vary significantly by street and locality.
- Follow local signs, temporary traffic arrangements, and directions from authorized officers.

## Notes
Treat cycling-specific points in this entry as safety and courtesy guidance unless a cited rule explicitly establishes a legal requirement. For current route safety, check live local conditions and ask accommodation staff or local authorities.
